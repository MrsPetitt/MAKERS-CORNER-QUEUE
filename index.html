Laser Cutting Details & Safety Procedures

Selected Machine: XTOOL

Safety Required

Safety Procedures & Rules

  • After every single job, clean out the build plate and pan.
  • If you have not used the laser cutter, ask for help and do not start a cut alone.
  • If you are unsure of any settings, ask for help and do not start a cut alone.
  • Leave the door shut while it is working; the laser will damage your eyes!
  • Turn off the machine before you walk away!
  • Do not crowd around the laser cutter; a maximum of two students may monitor a cut at one time.
  • People crowding around the maker’s corner will be told to leave.
  • No horseplay in the maker’s corner.

Remember, the laser cutter uses an actual LASER, which can be a risk for fire.

The XTOOL is a machine to use with care and respect.

Not following laser cutting procedures will result in detention and may result in a ban on access to the maker’s corner.

IN THE EVENT OF A FIRE, USE THE FIRE BLANKET TO COVER THE FLAMES.

You must select "Yes" to acknowledge the laser safety rules before joining the queue.
